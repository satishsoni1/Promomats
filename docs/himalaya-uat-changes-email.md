**Subject:** VODO PromoMats — your UAT feedback is done (all 37 items) + 4 quick questions

Hi Prathamesh and team,

Thank you for the detailed UAT feedback. Every item on the stakeholder list and
on Prathamesh's observation sheet has been built and tested. The full item-by-item
report is attached. The main changes you'll notice:

**Approval flow**
- New Draft stages: Content Manager, then Content Creator (Design Team). After
  that come Content Manager Review, TM/AGM, MLR (Medical, Regulatory and Legal at
  the same time) and Chairperson.
- When you start a task, you pick the person for each stage (e.g. Jalba or
  Rathna, the right TM/AGM, the brand's MLR reviewers). You can add someone
  outside the usual list, and set each stage's due time (48 hours by default).
- "Approved with changes" now comes back to the task owner. You can upload the
  revision yourself or send it to the Design Team. Once it's in, it moves
  straight on to the next stage instead of restarting.
- You can reassign a pending task if someone is unavailable, and anyone working
  a stage (except MLR) can upload a revised file.
- Brand Managers start, upload and assign tasks. They no longer get
  Approve/Reject.

**Finding and seeing work**
- A new **Active Workflow** page shows everything waiting on you. It has a
  separate view per Brand Manager and a Design Team queue where the team can pick
  up work or assign it to a designer.
- Jobs in progress are now visible only to their stakeholders. Approved material
  stays in the library for everyone.
- New Print / Digital classification with a Type of collateral list (LBL, LBC,
  Retailer Poster, VAF, Stockists Poster, In-/Out-Clinic Visibility, Dangler…).
- Placeholder jobs: create the task without a file, and the Design Team uploads
  the artwork.

**Reviewing**
- Creatives open in full view, with fullscreen. JPG/PNG artwork now opens in the
  viewer too.
- Comments sit in a side panel, like Adobe, so nothing covers the artwork.
- You can highlight text or draw a box around any part of the creative, suggest
  replacement text, and edit your own comments.
- Word files can be edited on the platform with Track Changes. PDFs can be
  downloaded with all comments included.

**Alerts, login and support**
- No password prompt when recording a decision, and you stay signed in for a
  full working day.
- Email reminders 12 hours before a task is due, and again if it becomes overdue.
- A Helpdesk in the sidebar for issues or change requests. Each ticket gets a
  reference number and a response time.
- A mobile app (Android and iOS) to review and approve assigned tasks from your
  phone.
